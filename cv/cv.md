Product-minded full-stack engineer and team lead with 10+ years of experience building beautiful, user-friendly, performant, and accessible websites, apps, design systems, and APIs with TypeScript, Ruby, and Haskell.
BSc and MSc in Computer Science, ETH Zürich.

# Employment

### Since 2025: Senior Full-Stack Engineer at [Everyman](https://everyman.health/), Zürich

- As the third engineer at the fast-growing medical startup, I brought down accumulated technical debt significantly (e.g. reduced website build time from 3min to 8secs, and bundle size from 1MB to 90kB), while at the same time adding new features cutting through both the front- and back-ends of the patient-facing website/app, as well as the internal platform for doctors, pharmacists and customer support. (Astro, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Claude AI, etc.)

### 2023-2025: CTO at [Spatz](https://www.spatz.news) (formerly Polaris News), Zürich

- Startup building a user-generated hyper-local news platform, including a chatbot for user-generated content submission. We started the company from scratch, and following the lean startup methodology, iterated through several versions of the product, quickly scaling up to a dozen regions. I hired two people, and in close collaboration with marketing and design, built the product. (Astro, SolidJS, Kysely, Supabase, etc.)

### 2019-2023: Team Lead Front-End at [Tamedia](https://www.tamedia.ch), Zürich

- Tamedia is one of the largest media companies in Switzerland. I led a team of 11 developers responsible for the platform delivering 13 websites, including [www.tagesanzeiger.ch](https://www.tagesanzeiger.ch). Our front-end service handled a sustained load of 150k reqs/min, with peaks exceeding 500k. Within Tamedia, our team pioneered a CI/CD workflow with feature-branch-deployments, and auto-deployment to production.

- I helped grow the skill-sets of my team members, and by building trust, significantly improved communication and processes between various teams (design, CMS, analytics, etc). One of the first things I did was to move our team and our Next.js codebase from JavaScript to TypeScript. One of the last things was to move the development of our native apps in-house: I hired one iOS and one Android dev, re-architected the apps as hybrid apps (Swift/Kotlin plus WebViews) and as promised, launched within a year.

### 2018-2019: Software Engineer at _[Fision Technologies](https://web.archive.org/web/20191225205351/https://www.fision-technologies.com/)_ (since acquired by Zalando), Zürich

- Fision Technologies was a startup offering an app to take photos of your body, from which a 3D-model was constructed, which was used to give size-recommendations for clothes when online-shopping. I worked on both the React front-end, and Python back-end services.

### 2015-2018: Cofounder, CTO and Full-Stack Developer of [2324.ch](https://web.archive.org/web/20250227191627/http://2324.ch/), Zürich

- My cofounder (a graphic designer) and I won an Impact Hub Zürich Fellowship with the idea of 2324.ch – a participatory hyper-local online news-platform. We quickly acquired four paying municipalities. Unfortunately, that was not enough to sustain our growth vision, so we decided to sell our company to [Crossiety](https://www.crossiety.ch/), a startup sharing our vision of an online village square.

- In addition to forming our team and product strategy, I implemented both front- and back-end. My decision to go with a simple and proven stack (Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL and some React) served us well, and allowed us to quickly iterate on the product.
